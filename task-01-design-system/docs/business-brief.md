# Nit's Cut — Business Brief

*Note: This brief was written retrospectively on 2026-10-30 after the initial Figma colour, typography, and shadow foundations were created. It records the learner's real apprenticeship observations and explicit design assumptions rather than a recorded customer interview.*

## The Business
**Nit's Cut** is a fictional Nigerian men's bespoke tailoring business making native wear, trousers, and shirts. While learning tailoring from my boss, I saw deadlines slip for two main reasons: power outages halting machine and pressing work, and too many orders piled onto one tailor without capacity tracking. Nit's Cut keeps client measurements, garment orders, stage progress, fitting dates, and payment records in one place.

## Who Uses It
The web app serves both **customers** and **the tailor**:
- **Age range:** 16+ years (working assumption, not from formal user interviews).
- **Device:** Mobile-first web app on Android and iOS phones.
- **Connection quality:** Unstable Nigerian mobile networks requiring lightweight pages.
- **Technical comfort:** Everyday smartphone users (WhatsApp and mobile banking), while staying clear for less tech-savvy users via plain labels and obvious actions.

## The One Thing That Must Work
**Seeing an accurate delivery date and live order progress.** When power outages or heavy workloads cause delays, customer and tailor must see the true garment stage and revised collection date.

## Three Questions Only Nit's Cut Can Answer
1. **“What stage is my outfit at?”** — Waiting, being cut, being sewn, or ready for collection.
2. **“When is my fitting, and when can I collect my outfit?”** — Agreed dates, changes, and delay reasons.
3. **“How much have I paid, and how much do I still owe?”** — Total price, recorded deposits, and balance owed.

## Required Feel and Why It Drives Typography and Colour
The interface must feel **minimalistic and dependable** for bespoke men's tailoring:
- **Brand colour (`#334DAF`):** Selected from a six-blue palette credited to `@The_Pensiler` so order and payment records feel dependable.
- **Typography (`Cormorant Garamond Semibold` + `Manrope`):** `Cormorant Garamond Semibold` gives Display and Headline styles **class, elegance, and beauty**. `Manrope` gives Title, Body, and Label styles a **modern, minimalistic look and balance**, keeping stages, dates, and figures readable on mobile.
