### Step 8 Three-Sentence Argument for Token Referencing

When we changed the `--nc-ref-primary-*` palette at the source from royal blue (`#334DAF` at tone 40) to emerald green (`#006D3B` at tone 40), every role still referencing the palette via `var()`—including `--nc-color-primary` on the `NC` badge, eyebrow, active-stage border, `Active` pill, and primary button, plus `--nc-color-on-primary-container` on the active stage text—updated automatically across the screen. Because `--nc-color-primary-container` had been broken to point at the raw hex `#213DA0` instead of `var(--nc-ref-primary-30)`, the `Being sewn` status pill and active stage card background stayed stuck in the old royal blue underneath green borders, green badges, and mint-green text (`11-broken-referencing.png`). That visual split is the whole argument for token referencing: when roles alias primitives and components consume only roles, a palette or theme change updates the entire interface from one place instead of leaving orphaned colours behind or forcing a manual search-and-replace across the codebase.
