Hard means sharper, not simply larger. These are custom brand shadows, not Material elevation presets.

Blue shadows add a cool brand tint around pale surfaces. Black shadows reduce brightness without that blue hue; compare against the actual background before choosing.

Shadows do not replace visible focus or essential boundaries. In dark mode use surface contrast and outlines because shadows may disappear.
