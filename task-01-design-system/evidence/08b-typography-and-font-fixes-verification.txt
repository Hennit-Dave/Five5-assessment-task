## 1. Investigation of the Original Screenshot (`10a-screen-with-system-before-fixes.png`)

In `08-agent-typography-answer.txt` (Point 3.1), the agent claimed that `10-screen-with-system.png` (now preserved as `10a-screen-with-system-before-fixes.png`, SHA-256 `c5e0252e7a3f3aeeb12df7cab22c402780df9a06d5513eb0a9853409b143757d`) had rendered the fallback font `Georgia` instead of `Cormorant Garamond` and `system-ui` instead of `Manrope`.

**Why that claim was unsupported and wrong:**
- The agent had checked `/Library/Fonts` and `~/Library/Fonts` (where neither font is installed locally) and noticed old-style descending digits (`3` and `5`) on `₦35,000`, assuming from those two observations that headless Chrome had failed to load Google Fonts before capturing.
- When inspected directly in headless Chrome via Chrome DevTools Protocol (`tokens/capture-and-verify-screen.mjs`, `DOM.enable` + `CSS.enable` + `CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode`), the original unedited page showed:
  - `failedNetworkRequests: []` (the remote Google Fonts stylesheet and `.woff2` subsets returned HTTP 200 when Chrome ran outside the sandbox).
  - `document.fonts.status: "loaded"`, with `document.fonts.check('600 32px "Cormorant Garamond"') === true`, `document.fonts.check('400 16px "Manrope"') === true`, and `document.fonts.check('600 16px "Manrope"') === true`.
  - `h1.hero-title` (*"Two-Piece Senator Native Wear & Fitted Trousers"*) rendered `47` glyphs using `CormorantGaramondLight-SemiBold` (`isCustomFont: true`), with an inline text width of `629.58px` (matching `Cormorant Garamond` `600` at `32px`: `629.57px`, whereas `Georgia` `600` at `32px` measures `816.03px`).
  - `dd.amount-owed` (*"₦35,000"*) rendered `7` glyphs using `CormorantGaramondLight-SemiBold` (`isCustomFont: true`).
  - Why `₦35,000` displayed descending digits `3` and `5`: `Cormorant Garamond` uses proportional old-style numerals (`onum`) by default. They appeared on `₦35,000` only because `screen/index.html` had misapplied `.nc-type-headline-small` (`Cormorant Garamond`) to that payment balance (Point 3.2 of `08-agent-typography-answer.txt`).

- **Remaining glyph coverage limitation (recorded in `docs/defects.md`):**

Consequently, on `₦85,000`, `− ₦50,000`, and `₦35,000`, the `₦` currency symbol (`1` glyph) falls back to the system sans-serif font (`.SF NS` on macOS) while all numeric digits and punctuation render in `Manrope-SemiBold`.
- **Colour-token checks (`evidence/07-raw-value-search.txt` and `tokens/validate-css.mjs`):**
